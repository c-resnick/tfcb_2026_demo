## Procedure
Not included here are details on making IGM and cell culture media. Refer to Bloom lab notebook that is not accessible by this course for these details. 
### Setup sera on plate
  We start with a 1:40 sera dilution in the top row and then do 2.3-fold dilutions down the plate.

1. Add 70.8uL IGM to the first row (A), wells 1-11. 
2. Add 50uL IGM to all other wells (A12 + rest of plate)
3. Add 17.7uL serum to the first row (A1-11)
4. Move 38.5uL from sera-containing wells in row A down the plate, pipetting up/down gently 3-5x. Change tips between wells
5. Leave no serum control wells untouched

### Make virus library dilution
Make a 1:64 dilution of the flu-seqneut-2026 virus library:

1. 80 uL virus library pool + 5040uL IGM, pipette to mix with a serological
2. transfer mixture to 
3. Using a p200 multichannel, add 50uL of virus stock to each well. Mix gently 2x.
  Change tips every row
4. Incubate at 37C for 1 hour ~3PM
